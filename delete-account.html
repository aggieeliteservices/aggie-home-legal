Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Aggie Home account and any data associated with it. Aggie Home is an internal field-operations app used by employees and contractors of Aggie Home Solutions, LLC.

How to request deletion of your account

1. Email aggiehomesolutions1@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to the app.
2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
3. Include your full name and (if you are a service customer) the address associated with your service so we can locate your record.

We will respond within seven (7) business days to confirm receipt, and will complete the deletion within forty-five (45) days of the request.

What is deleted

When you request a full account deletion, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your login credentials (email address, hashed password)
  • Your profile (name, phone number, role, hourly rate)
  • Your time-clock history
  • Your in-app voice transcripts and chat history
  • Your push-notification token
  • Your door-canvassing pins

What is retained

The following data is retained, in compliance with US tax, employment, and consumer-protection law, for up to seven (7) years after the date of deletion:

  • Commission and payroll records that are subject to IRS retention rules
  • Signed service contracts you created (with personally-identifying fields redacted where the underlying contract permits)
  • Aggregate, non-identifying analytics used to operate and improve the app

Service-customer records (the homeowners and property managers we serve) are governed separately by our Privacy Policy. Customers may also email the address above to request deletion of their own records.

Partial deletion

If you wish to delete some of your data but keep your account active — for example, you want to remove old photos or clear your door-canvassing pin history without losing your employee profile — email us with a description of what you want removed. We process partial deletion requests on the same forty-five (45) day timeline.
